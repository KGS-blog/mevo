How We Work

A disciplined four-step approach from brief to delivery — with quality control at every stage.

Quality Control Designed for Real-World Fieldwork

We apply practical, risk-based checks that fit the study’s method, timeline, and budget.

Pre-Field
  • Review target profile, quota, screener, and fieldwork instructions
  • Confirm respondent eligibility criteria and recruitment source
  • Check questionnaire flow and practical feasibility
  • Align escalation rules before data collection begins
In-Field
  • Monitor quota, completion volume, dropout, and respondent patterns
  • Control respondent frequency to reduce over-participation
  • Flag suspiciously fast, repetitive, incomplete, or inconsistent responses
  • Escalate unusual patterns before they affect the full sample
  • Conduct targeted spot-checks when a risk signal appears
Post-Field
  • Review duplicate, inconsistent, incomplete, or low-quality cases
  • Compare fieldwork output against the agreed quota and criteria
  • Document exclusions, replacements, and material deviations
  • Deliver a concise QC summary with the final data
